# Defect or Audit Gap? Why Referee Forces a Call Anyway

Date: 2025-10-15

Research criticism usually collapses actual flaws and missing information into one vague complaint. Referee separates defects from audit gaps and then forces explicit yes/no calls on both. Ambiguity is preserved in the evidence record, not promoted into a third scoring state.
